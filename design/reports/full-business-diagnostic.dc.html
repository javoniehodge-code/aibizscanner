What do “Search” and “Read” mean?

“Search” is whether the page came back when the AI searched for it. “Read” is whether the AI’s own reading tool could get the page’s content from the exact link. “Partly read” means it got the page but missed key content, usually because that content only loads with JavaScript. We record the two separately: a page can be readable but hard to find, or found but not fully readable.

RAW VS FULLY LOADED

What AI sees before and after your pages finish loading

ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity read a page’s raw code and don’t wait for content that loads afterward with JavaScript. Google, and so Gemini, loads the full page. Anything that only appears after loading is invisible to most AI.

20BEST PRACTICES{{ biz.name }}{{ biz.location }}

General best practices

Simple, ongoing habits that help any business show up accurately in AI answers, beyond the specific fixes in your checklist.

1Monitor visits from AI toolsCheck how many visitors arrive from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and other assistants—and which pages they visit. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is a good, free starting point.
2Track what those visitors doMeasure inquiries, bookings, purchases, and other valuable actions. Traffic alone doesn’t tell you whether AI is bringing customers. GA4 can track these actions for free.
3Ask customers how they found youAdd “AI assistant, such as ChatGPT” to your inquiry or checkout survey. This can capture discovery that never produces a directly attributed website visit.
4Keep business information accurate everywhereCheck your name, location, phone number, hours, services, prices, and booking links across your website and important third-party profiles. Update them whenever something changes.
5Claim and complete relevant business profilesStart with Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, and the review or industry platforms relevant to your business. Complete the useful fields; remove outdated information and duplicate listings.
6Put essential decision facts on your websiteClearly state what you offer, where you operate, who you serve, and relevant details such as accessibility, parking, qualifications, pricing, or how quotes work.
7Answer real customer questionsPublish direct answers to questions customers ask before buying: “Do you handle emergencies?” “Is parking included?” “Can you accommodate large groups?”
8Make important information easy to readPut essential facts in ordinary webpage text. If your menu, service list, or pricing exists only in an image or downloadable brochure, add a readable webpage version.
9Check that search engines and AI search crawlers can access your siteAsk your web developer to check crawler access, indexing, and security settings that might block legitimate crawlers.
10Get more useful customer reviewsMake asking for honest reviews part of your normal process. Focus on platforms relevant to your customers and address recurring problems identified in feedback.
11Prioritize third-party sites AI actually usesLook at sources cited for your industry and customer questions. Check whether your business appears there and whether the information is accurate and useful.
12Improve the quality of existing mentionsAn old listing with only your name is less useful to a customer than a current page explaining your services, location, strengths, and supporting evidence. Request corrections or fuller details where appropriate.
13Earn credible coverage and recommendationsPursue relevant local publications, trade organizations, expert roundups, partner pages, and industry directories. Choose sources with a real audience and editorial purpose.
14Make your strengths specific and verifiableReplace vague claims like “excellent service” with evidence: qualifications, amenities, project examples, supported service areas, or clearly explained policies.
15Track the same customer questions over timeRegularly check whether AI mentions you, recommends you, describes you accurately, and cites your website. Use a consistent set of questions and look for patterns across repeated checks.
16Keep a simple improvement logRecord what changed and when: profile corrections, new service pages, reviews, or press coverage. Review visibility, traffic, and leads alongside those changes without assuming every movement was caused by them.
