# Credible & corroborated implementation

See the [moving company reference](../product/ai-brand-perception/sections/credible-corroborated/README.md) and [data contract](../product/ai-brand-perception/sections/credible-corroborated/data-contract.md). This section assesses independent support for the business. It consumes AI Prompt Analysis findings rather than running a default set of named-business claim checks.

1. **Select relevant prompts.** Use a compatible Prompt Analysis run and filter to the relevant service, market and recommendation intents. Retain eligible completed response and citation coverage. The broader decision rules for reuse and standalone runs are a [build-plan to-do](../planning/build-plan.md#to-do--brand-audit-inputs-from-ai-prompt-analysis).
2. **Rank cited pages.** Normalize exact page URLs without merging distinct content. Deduplicate each page within each prompt across providers and trials, then sort by distinct prompt count. Preserve all citation events and show the providers observed for each page. Rank does not establish endorsement, source quality or influence.
3. **Inspect the exact pages.** Check for the correct business entity, how the page describes it, concrete recommendation reasons and matched competitors. Preserve excerpts, page scope and retrieval states. A failed fetch means could not check. Distinguish sources that recommend the business from sources that merely name it or recommend competitors.
4. **Assess reviews.** Inspect relevant cited profiles and a small bounded set of relevant profiles checked separately. Match business and location. Record rating, volume, recency, sample coverage and supported positive and negative themes. Do not count separately checked profiles as AI citations or count syndicated evidence as independent.
5. **Summarize support and actions.** Explain what gives customers a reason to choose the business, where support is thin, and which coverage gaps to prioritize. Flag sparse citations rather than assuming the rest of the web offers no support. Cite inspected pages and profile evidence for recommendations. Route incorrect business facts to Consistent & Current.
6. **Keep evidence inspectable.** Link prompt and source records; distinguish cited and separately checked data. If showing change over time, compare compatible runs or matching review profiles and label coverage differences. Keep synthetic fixtures out of production.

Acceptance checks: repeated citations of one page do not inflate prompt counts; multiple pages from one domain stay distinct; all observed provider labels are retained; separately checked reviews do not affect citation counts; competitor-only coverage, plain mentions and recommendations are distinct; unavailable pages never imply absence; examples remain visibly fictional.
