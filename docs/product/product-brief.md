# Product brief
Version 0.3 • 22 October 2026

## Promise
Help local and online businesses understand how AI describes them, when it recommends them, which competitors appear instead and how geographic wording changes results. Translate evidence into a practical, rechecked owner checklist.
Use a plain-English homepage that progresses from understanding to recommendations, competition, geography and ongoing progress.

## Products and access
| Product | Purpose | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Prompt Analysis | Approved-prompt visibility, competitors and citations | Five-prompt free version; expanded paid report |
| Brand Strength Analysis | Open-ended AI perspectives plus five evidence buckets and actions | Paid |
| Competitive Analysis | Compare the target with up to four competitors using the brand evidence framework and stated rationales | Paid |
| Location Analysis | Matched questions across locations with competitors and citations | Paid |
| Monitoring dashboard | History, prioritized tracked prompts and discrepancy checklist | Subscription |

All four reports are launch scope. Dashboard belongs in the product/design scope; its release timing and exact plan economics are open. One-off and timed report bundles are intended; they are distinct from dashboard prompt monitoring.
Every paid purchase requires an account. Only the free visibility report is available to guests. Free results are visible before optional email delivery or signup; no free Brand Strength report.
Product pages disclose what buyers need to decide before purchase. Paid flow: product → name/email/business details and signup/sign-in → payment → remaining setup/profile confirmation → approve questions → scan → results.

## Business profile and priorities
Import publicly available facts with source/date and let the owner approve/correct them. Keep observations and owner-confirmed facts distinct.
Separately capture locations, selected competitors, desired positioning, priority services/products, goals and preferred customer needs. Priorities inform prompt suggestions, category emphasis and ordering, not factual claims or forced AI recommendations.
Keep approved question sets reusable across reports. Pin/reorder without changing history; new wording/language/location versions create distinct test configurations.

## Evidence and advice
- Tested findings are actual recorded mentions, recommendations and citations.
- Brand analysis asks each AI openly when/why it would recommend or hesitate; named-brand suitability is not unbranded discovery.
- Five buckets assess accessible business information, independent validation, customer review evidence, expertise/supporting evidence and information currency.
- Green/yellow/red/gray statuses use documented criteria, evidence and next steps. Gray covers insufficient evidence or not applicable under distinct labels.
- Creative discovery opportunities need not have verified search demand but remain labeled ideas.
- Stated rationales and suggested improvements do not prove internal ranking mechanisms or causal effects.

See [report catalog](report-catalog.md) and [brand/checklist specification](brand-evidence-and-checklist.md).

## Monitoring
Prompt checks run three times per week, using saved question versions and declared provider settings. These are scheduled samples, not real-time monitoring.
Brand, competitive and location analyses refresh more slowly: monthly versus twice monthly is unresolved. Do not run full analyses at every prompt check.
Plot actual observations, missing data and clearly labeled trend summaries. Record owner changes without claiming causation. Display each module's last/next refresh.
Recheck checklist items only when applicable evidence is actually collected. Failed/missing checks cannot complete tasks.

## Checklist
Separate website/profile corrections the owner can make from AI-answer discrepancies awaiting later observations. Owner-confirmed facts do not overwrite evidence.
Statuses: Open, Owner marked done—awaiting verification, Verified complete, Could not verify. Non-verifiable strategic tasks can be Owner completed, explicitly distinct.
Keep completed history and verification dates. Reopen recurring issues. Never close a task merely because it disappeared from one generated summary.

## Commercial decisions still open
Prices; paid/free repetition counts; paid prompt and business/location caps; free rate limits; bundle timing/credits; subscription scope/price/cancellation; monthly vs twice-monthly full analyses; retention and refund terms; release timing of dashboard/bundles; final brand/domain.
Free prompt limit is fixed at five and competitor comparison is capped at up to four competitors for the paid Competitive Analysis. Fifty tracked prompts is an illustrative planning example, not a confirmed plan cap.

## Carry-forward requirements
Direct OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and Perplexity API integration; no OpenRouter or Google AI Overview collection in MVP. English/Spanish implementation default, one scan language at a time; PDF and verified email delivery; bounded beta waivers.
Agency packs, native social collection and integrations/plugin marketplace are separate later work. GBP connection is a candidate requiring separate access/permission work, not part of the design skeleton's live functionality.

## Success and limits
Track product-specific conversion, question edits, paid repeat use, completion/partial failure, costs, refunds, checklist verification and monitoring retention. Separate beta waivers from paying demand.
Measure generation/search, extraction, advice, fetches, retry, scheduler, storage, PDF/email and payment costs by product and cadence before charging.
No global AI ranking, market-share claim, arbitrary readiness score or guaranteed recommendation improvement.
