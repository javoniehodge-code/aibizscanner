How the labels work

Named: the business is substantively identified, including neutral or negative mentions.

Recommended: the answer presents the business as a suitable choice, including a conditional recommendation for a stated need.

Explicit first choice: the answer directly identifies it as the top choice for the question or a stated need. First appearance alone does not qualify.

Fit and concerns: short supported descriptions, rather than forced chart categories. A recommendation can coexist with a concern.

A production report links every classification to an answer excerpt, preserves unclear cases and separates unavailable answers. This design example assumes all answers are usable and identities resolved.
