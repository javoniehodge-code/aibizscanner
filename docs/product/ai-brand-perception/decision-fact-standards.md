# Shared decision-fact standards

Confirmed 23 October 2026. Applies to **Clear & complete** and **Consistent & current** in the AI Brand Perception Audit.

## Rules

Use **5–10 decision facts total per business type**, with ten as a ceiling, not a target. There are only two buckets: **Core** and **When relevant**. Exclude conditional facts that do not apply. Do not add buying-context or usually-unnecessary buckets to the report. A fact belongs if getting it wrong could change the recommendation or make the purchase unsuitable.

Version standards by industry/subtype. Store applicability, required subfields and essential qualifiers behind each fact without expanding the report into many more rows. Section 2 asks whether the website supplies sufficient clear, concise and complete detail. Section 3 asks whether the same facts agree across sources and still apply. Neither is proof of a provider's actual recommendation.

## Boutique hotel — approved standard v1.0

| Bucket | ID | Decision fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | location | Location and neighborhood |
| Core | price-fees | Price range and mandatory fees |
| Core | rooms-occupancy | Room types and occupancy |
| Core | amenities | Amenities and their availability |
| Core | atmosphere | Atmosphere and guest fit |
| When relevant | parking | Parking and charges |
| When relevant | pets | Pet policy and charges |
| When relevant | accessibility | Specific accessibility features |
| When relevant | breakfast | Breakfast and inclusions |
| When relevant | booking-terms | Cancellation and booking restrictions |

Atmosphere consistency compares descriptions; supporting the claimed experience belongs in credibility/reviews. Price comparisons must distinguish general price positioning, fee policies and date-specific quotes. Accessibility must use specific features, not a vague accessible label.

## Coffee — approved standard v1.0

| Bucket | ID | Decision fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | flavor | Flavor profile |
| Core | roast | Roast level |
| Core | format-grind | Format and grind |
| Core | brewing-compatibility | Brewing suitability or machine compatibility |
| Core | caffeine | Regular or decaf |
| When relevant | value | Price relative to quantity or yield |
| When relevant | ingredients | Ingredients and additives |
| When relevant | certifications-sourcing | Certifications and sourcing claims |

Apply facts to relevant products/variants; do not require ground coffee to have capsule compatibility. For widely distributed coffee sold online, local store stock and nearby retailers are not standard decision facts. Product format still matters to suitability. Supported flavor description and substantiation of flavor quality are different assessments.

## Migration of the earlier coffee example

The earlier approved Section 2 visual remains an archived design reference with eight recommendation questions and synthetic 4/2/2 outcomes. Its checklist predates these shared standards. New production coffee reports must use this standard: omit the old purchase-route row as a routine decision fact, combine brewing/machine suitability, and apply ingredients and certification/sourcing rows only when relevant. Do not silently transform the old fixture or carry its scores into a new standard. A refreshed visual/fixture must be explicitly labeled with the new standard version and recomputed evidence/outcomes.

## Engineering handoff

The category facts and two-bucket limit are confirmed product decisions. Required subfield definitions, source sampling and assessment resolution are versioned implementation proposals; do not claim every implementation default was individually approved. Add new industry templates through reviewed standards, not an unconstrained model-generated checklist.
