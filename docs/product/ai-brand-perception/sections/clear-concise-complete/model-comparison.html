What should improve next?

Shared website gaps

Clarify factual flavor distinctions and regular/decaf labeling. Connect price, quantity and yield where a value comparison is relevant.

Model-specific gaps

Recheck Claude and Gemini on the explicit machine-system statement. Preserve clear sourcing qualifiers for every model. A wrong answer despite clear website evidence is a model-output issue; the report should not automatically blame missing website content.

Website sufficiency and AI answer quality are separate measures. Correctly admitting uncertainty avoids an invented claim, but does not count as a completed product answer. The six selected pages are not the total website.
